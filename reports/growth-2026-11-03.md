(customer reviews, review screen, review record, price changes, price tags, shelf manager, restock manager,
cleaners); the new guide is the connective route, which is the long-tail shape this cluster was missing. No
Search Console export is available in this workspace, so this is a coverage-gap choice, not a claim about
impressions.
