## This pass

**Cluster: workers and automatic restocking (员工与自动补货).** One new indexable guide:

- `/articles/which-shop-jobs-go-to-a-worker-a-machine-or-the-restocker` — "Which shop jobs go to a worker, a
  machine or the restocker". 10 steps, 7 common mistakes. Every step is grounded in facts already published on the
  entry pages and in the existing guides; no new numbers and no invented mechanics.

**Why this is the gap.** The site already had one guide for each lane of the work: the restock cart and screens
(`restocking-without-guessing-what-you-need`, `sorting-the-restock-list-before-you-spend`), directing a worker
(`getting-your-workers-doing-what-you-want`), and the machines plus the restocker
(`letting-the-machines-and-the-restocker-do-the-work`). What it did not have was the **handoff path between the
lanes**: which job belongs to the restocker, which to a worker, which to the automatic machines, and which stays
with the owner. The new guide is that connective route. It does not re-teach the restock screen order, the worker
screen fields or the machine states; it points at those guides and answers the division-of-labour question between
them.
