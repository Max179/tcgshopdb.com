# Image review log

Every image this site shows as a picture from the game is exported from this installation by
`tools/extract-images.py`, hashed when it is written, and listed with its origin in
`content/images-manifest.json`. This log records the separate, human step: opening the exported file and
checking by eye that it is a legible picture of the thing the entry says it is.

A picture fails the review if it is near-blank or featureless (a flat colour, an all-black or all-white
sheet, or unreadable stripes), or if what it actually shows is not the thing the note claims. A failed
picture is deleted together with its mapping and the manifest is regenerated; the entry then either gets a
different picture or is labelled as carrying an original diagram only. A verdict of "ok" means the file was
opened, compared against its note and its entry, and kept.

## Sanity pass before the eye check (tools/image-sanity.py)

`tools/image-sanity.py` measures every exported file first: greyscale, with transparent pictures composited on the dark page background, flagging a file when it carries almost no variation (stddev below 12) or when more than 90% of its pixels sit within 6 of the median. The run for this build flagged 7 of 71 records, and each one is a case where the measurement needs the entry read beside it:

- **what-a-ghost-card-does-ghost.jpg** (98%) and **what-a-card-shows-in-play-icon.jpg** (98%) — icon sheets: four small icons on the game's own black field. The icons are readable and were logged above. Kept.
- **albums-and-binder-pages-binder.jpg** (stddev 7.7) — the dark binder page already logged above; its pockets, seam and ring holes are visible. Kept.
- **the-finishes-you-can-buy-wall-blue.jpg** (stddev 2.0) and **the-finishes-you-can-buy-wall-orange.jpg** (stddev 1.7) — flat colour sheets, which is exactly what a flat-coloured wall finish is; the notes say they are colour textures for a wall finish. Kept, and recorded here as flat by design rather than blank: the picture's whole content is the colour the finish has.
- **playmats-deck-boxes-and-sleeves-deck-box.jpg** (stddev 8.1) — the dark red deck box with the Deckbox marking and the Tetramon logo; the marking and the box edges are visible. Kept.
- **the-box-your-stock-arrives-in-cardboard.jpg** (93%) — a flat red box, which is what a box is; its delivery label, CE and recycling marks and handling icons are readable. Kept.

The run is written to `reports/image-sanity.md` on every build; a flag is a request for a look, not a verdict.
